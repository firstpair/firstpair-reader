# One syllable at a time

A syllable is a run of letters followed by a digit from 1 to 5. `ü` may be typed as `u:` or `v`.

```firstpair-fragment
{"id": "SYLLABLE"}
```

Each match is rewritten on its own; the fifth (neutral) tone keeps its letters and loses the digit.

```firstpair-fragment
{"id": "convert_syllable"}
```
