# A small shelf

**[Open the Reader](firstpair:reader)**

Three books, each in its own folder with its own `_data/reader.json`. The Reader opens on a shelf listing them; each book keeps its own place.

- [[Analects/Home|Лунь юй · Беседы и суждения]]: Chinese → Russian and English, Russian interface, glosses in Russian and English
- [[Diary of a Madman/Home|A Madman's Diary]]: Chinese → English, the Chinese a study column you switch on, pinyin and English glosses
- [[Gettysburg Address/Home|The Gettysburg Address]]: a plain English book cut into pages from one note
