## Entry I

### I

Tonight the moonlight is very fine.

I had not seen it for more than thirty years; seeing it today, I feel unusually clear-headed. Only now do I know that those thirty-odd years were spent in a daze. But I must be very careful. Otherwise, why would the Zhaos' dog have looked at me twice?

I have good reason to be afraid.
