# About the texts

- **Original**: chapters 1–3 of the *Dao De Jing* in the received text with Wang Bi's commentary (third century), in simplified characters; public domain.
- **James Legge**: *The Texts of Taoism*, Sacred Books of the East, vol. 39 (Oxford, 1891); public domain. His parenthetical additions are kept.
- **Sample translation**: written for these samples and released with them under the MIT licence.
