# The Ferry at Low Water

**[[The Ferry Crossing/Triptychs/Reader|Open the Triptych Reader]]** · **[[The Ferry Crossing/Triptychs/Review|Open Review]]**

A sample vault for the FirstPair Triptych plugin: three drafts of one short essay, side by side.

- **Draft 1**, **Draft 2**, **Final**: the drafts differ by word choice (*town* becomes *village*, *ferryman* becomes *boatman*), punctuation, a dropped sentence (page 4), added sentences (pages 2 and 5, and a closing page found only in the Final), and a sentence moved from page 2 to page 3.
- Two sets: *All drafts* (three panes) and *Last revision* (Draft 2 against Final).
- Each draft whole: [[The Ferry Crossing/Editions/Draft 1|Draft 1]], [[The Ferry Crossing/Editions/Draft 2|Draft 2]], [[The Ferry Crossing/Editions/Final|Final]].

The essay was written for this sample and is released under the MIT License with the rest of the repository. The commits and versions are placeholders. Generated by `samples/build/build_triptych.py`; edit that script, not these notes.

To try it, install the plugin into this vault's `.obsidian/plugins/firstpair-triptych/` and enable it under Settings → Community plugins.
